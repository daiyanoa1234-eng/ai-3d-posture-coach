Three.js: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js — copyright is retained in three-MIT.txt.

MediaPipe Pose Landmarker models are from the Google official float16/1 model distribution.
Model license: Apache-2.0, as indicated by the official model card linked from the Pose Landmarker guide.
Model card (reference only, not bundled): https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/Model%20Card%20BlazePose%20GHUM%203D.pdf
