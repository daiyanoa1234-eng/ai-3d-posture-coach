This application bundles Three.js 0.180.0 (MIT) and MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.1.0 (Apache-2.0).
Three.js: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js — copyright is retained in three-MIT.txt.
MediaPipe: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe — Copyright Google LLC.
MediaPipe Pose Landmarker models are from the Google official float16/1 model distribution.
Model URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in models/manifest.json and scripts/prepare-assets.mjs.
The browser application does not transmit camera frames or pose landmarks.
